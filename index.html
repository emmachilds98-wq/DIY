Media Wall v11

The artisan oak build in Farrow & Ball Card Room Green, drawn to the room as you measured it. Switch between the media wall, the slat wall and the dining wall set for four, try the other greens, or turn on the numbered key.

What changed in v11: the TV moves 25 cm nearer the side wall, so the turntable fits in the alcove with the Series X on a shelf above it and the record trays in the bay below. The record ledge wraps round onto the side wall, with the PC on a shelf above the end of the L-sofa. The chimney breast gets a fold-down oak dining table for four (six or eight with it lifted off), a second spotlight bar goes on your existing point by the door, and the sub moves under the dining wall, on the front wall beside the TV.

Lighting

  • The LED strip you have now, on the ceiling round the whole room, facing down.
  • Two four-head cone spotlight bars, Wolston style. The first, on the existing rose between the TV and the sofa, lights the shelves, the ledge, the coffee table and the turntable. The second, on the existing point between the door and the wall, lights the table and the framed LPs above it.
  • LED grooves in the oak uprights and a bias light behind the TV. No spot ever points at the screen.
