Build guide: the whole wall

Everything needed to build the unit, the oak joinery, the trays and drawers, and the PC shelf. Sizes are finished sizes in centimetres. Measure your own alcove first: walls are rarely square, so take its width at the floor, at 76 cm, at 192 cm and at the ceiling, and cut the worktop and shelves to the smallest.

Tools

  • Drill-driver and an SDS or hammer drill for the masonry, with masonry bits and plugs.
  • Spirit level (a cross-line laser makes the shelf heights quick), tape, combination square, stud and cable detector.
  • Track or circular saw for the worktop and ply (or have the timber merchant cut the ply and oak to the cut lists), jigsaw for notches.
  • For the joints: a sharp set of chisels (6, 12 and 25 mm), a marking gauge, a tenon saw, a router with a straight bit for the housings and grooves, a 10 mm drill bit for the pegs, and a mallet.
  • Router with a cove bit for the finger pulls (or buy lips with the pull routed), clamps, sander, wood glue.
  • Dust mask, eye and ear protection.

Order of work

  1. Check and measure: ceiling height, alcove width at four heights, the walls for plumb, the floor for level across the alcove, and your records and games.
  2. Electrician: Cat6 runs (the one for Deco B through the ceiling in the alcove's right corner), swap the single socket behind bay 1 for a double, sockets by the sofa and under the PC shelf, the fused spur for the LED drivers, and the ceiling rose and dimmer for the pendant. Mark every cable route on the wall before painting.
  3. Paint the room in Sage Green, skirting and door in eggshell, alcove included, and let it cure for a few days before fixing anything to it.
  4. Mark heights on the walls with a level or laser: 73 (worktop battens), 37 (kit shelf battens), 109 (turntable shelf underside), 152 (left shelf underside), 188 (high shelf underside), and the uprights' lines at 44.5–49 and 175.5–180 cm from the left wall.
  5. Fix the cleats and battens on the side walls for the shelf ends and the sub bay, into the masonry, level. Cut the skirting back where the base meets the walls.
  6. Build the plinth and set it against the spacer blocks, shimmed dead level: it decides whether the worktop sits flat.
  7. Assemble the frames with their back panels off, stand them on the plinth, join them with BESTÅ connectors and screw them down to it.
  8. Build the bay 3 carcass, set it beside bay 2, shim it so its top is level with the frames at 73, bolt it to bay 2, and fit the anti-tip brackets on all three.
  9. Fit the worktop: scribe the ends to the walls, oil it all over (underside too, so it stays flat), and screw up through the frame and carcass tops in slotted holes (short screws that can't come through). Felt pads on the sub-bay battens.
  10. Fit the trunking in the channel behind the unit, mains on one side and data on the other, and run the cables in.
  11. Fit the internal shelves in bays 1 and 2 at the fit-check heights.
  12. Build and fit the drawers: both record drawers in bay 3, then the game drawer in bay 1 (steps in their sections).
  13. Fit the kit shelf and the hidden drawer in the sub bay, and put the sub on its riser.
  14. Cut the joints and fit the oak: dry-fit each upright with its shelves on the bench, then stand the uprights on their dowels, fix the concealed brackets, and fit the shelves, ledge, wedges and pegs (see "The oak joinery"). Lay the Deco leads in the right upright's groove before it goes up.
  15. Build the PC shelf box, fit the keyboard tray runners, hang it on its cleat and brackets, fit the screen and the trunking down to the skirting.
  16. Make the LP browser, fit the oak face frame, and finish all the oak with hardwax oil.
  17. Place and connect everything, label each plug and HDMI input, and test.

Then: lighting

  1. Upright grooves: aluminium channel and a 2700K strip in each upright's inner groove, wired down the back groove to a driver on the fused spur in the sub bay.
  2. Ceiling strip: re-stick the existing strip through the notches at the top of the uprights; add the channel and diffuser whenever you upgrade it.
  3. Bias light on the back of the TV, from its USB port.
  4. Pendant: hang the lantern on the new rose with the shortest drop that suits the headroom, and fit the dimmer.
