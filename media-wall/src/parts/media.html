Media wall proposal

One full-width unit across the alcove. Bays 1 and 2 are IKEA BESTÅ 60×40×64 frames on a 9 cm plinth; bay 3 is a custom plywood carcass the same height that holds two face-out record drawers; the 45 cm bay at the right stays open for the subwoofer. A worktop cut to 225 × 40 cm runs across the top at 75.8 cm, sideboard height. The TV stays on LG's own stand with the soundbar at the front edge, and a solid oak high shelf holds books, Deco B and a print. Each bay holds one family: Xbox under the Series X, PlayStation under the TV, vinyl under the turntable. Every console fits even at its largest version, every record is face-out, and every cable drops through a slot at the back into a hidden channel.

Built to your alcove: 225 cm wide, 50 cm deep, floor to ceiling (220 cm)

  • Bays 1 and 2: two BESTÅ frames, each 60 × 40 × 64 cm, stand on a 9 cm plywood plinth, so their tops are at 73 cm. Back panels off. The face frame's bottom rail covers the plinth, so it never shows.
  • Bay 3: a custom carcass in 18 mm birch ply, 60 × 40 × 73 cm, standing on the floor with its top level with the frames. Inside it is 56.4 cm wide and 69.4 cm tall: exactly two face-out tiers of 34.7 cm. The drawer runners screw straight into the ply sides, so it needs no liners.
  • Fixing: the frames and carcass are bolted to each other, stand 10 cm off the back wall on spacer blocks, and are fixed to it with anti-tip brackets. With two drawers of about 30 kg each, the anti-tip brackets on bay 3 are essential, and only one record drawer should be open at a time.
  • Worktop: a 2.8 cm laminate worktop cut to 225 × 40 cm, flush with the fronts, top at 75.8 cm. The 10 cm left open behind it (5.4 cm once the slats go on) is the slot for cables and rising warm air.
  • Inside bays 1 and 2: about 56.8 cm wide, 38–40 cm deep with the back open, and 60.8 cm high, exactly as before: every row and console just sits 9 cm higher. The game drawer's base is now 11.6 cm off the floor, easier to reach.
  • Matching fronts: the game drawer and both record drawers have 6.5 cm oak lips with a finger pull, so the bottom of the unit reads as one set, with covers on show in each.
  • TV: on LG's own stand (base 47 × 23 cm), set towards the back of the worktop. The bottom of the screen is about 5.4 cm above the worktop (81.2 cm), its centre at about 116.5 cm and its top at 151.7 cm. The C6's HDMI ports are on the left as you face it, the same side as the Series X and the PC's cable.
  • Soundbar: about 92 × 9 × 6 cm at the front edge, 8 cm in front of the TV stand. From the sofa you look down over it, so it stays below the picture, and the TV's remote sensor still works.
  • Series X: upright on its fan stand at the left end of the worktop, about 15 cm from the wall and the TV. Console and stand stand about 37 cm tall (to 112.4 cm), with open air above.
  • Turntable: on a 45 cm-deep oak wall shelf with its top at 113 cm in the right-hand gap, big enough for a deck as large as the Audio-Technica AT-LP120X (45 × 35 cm). It sits on a hidden cleat on the right-hand wall and a back batten, clear of the unit. Keep about 40 cm clear above the deck: the lid opens to about 165 cm.
  • LP browser: on the worktop under the turntable shelf, 33.2 cm clear (worktop 75.8 to the shelf's underside at 109). A 31.4 cm sleeve leaning back 10° stands about 31.7 cm on the browser's base, leaving 1.5 cm. At 48 × 24 cm it holds about 50 LPs face-out, about 13 kg, which the worktop carries over the sub bay.
  • Sub bay: open from the floor. The sub stands on a 5 cm riser (its top at 34.4 cm) under a kit shelf with its top at 38.8 cm. The shelf holds the preamp, spare stylus, cleaning kit and brush on the left and about 70 singles spines-out on the right, with 26.8 cm clear up to the hidden drawer.
  • Hidden slim drawer: under the worktop in the sub bay, an oak front 44 × 7.4 cm (65.6–73 cm) with a finger pull underneath, on a ply box 40 × 28 × 6 cm and 250 mm runners. Remotes, batteries, styli, spare cables.
  • PC: on an oak wall shelf about 65 × 32 cm, top at 110 cm, on the left wall about 1.3–2 m forward of the alcove, glass to the room, the back ports towards the sofa behind an oak screen, keyboard tray underneath. See "The PC shelf".
  • High shelf: solid oak, 225 × 30 × 4 cm, top at 192 cm, wall to wall on hidden cleats, with 28 cm to the ceiling: books up to 26 cm, a box for cables, a print and Deco B. The optional left shelf sits at 156 cm.

Why the unit is 76 cm tall

Two face-out drawers stacked need more height than a BESTÅ frame has inside, and the worktop has to stay level across the alcove because the TV spans bays 1 to 3. So the whole unit goes up 9 cm, from 67 to about 76 cm.

The record drawers

Bay 3, under the turntable, is two roll-out drawers stacked, and every LP in them is face-out. Records stand in a trough on the left of each drawer, leaning back about 10° like a record shop browser. With the drawer shut, the room sees the front cover above the oak lip. Pull it out and flip through the covers from the front, tip one forward and lift it out. A narrower compartment on the right holds 7-inch singles spine-up, with a stack of spare inner sleeves in front of them.

How the system works

  • LP trough: 33 cm wide, so a 31.4 cm sleeve has about 8 mm each side to drop in without scuffing. Records lean back 10° against each other; the last one leans on a raked oak rest at the back of the trough.
  • Capacity: the lean takes about 5.5 cm of the 37.6 cm inside depth, leaving about 30 cm of stack: about 85 LPs at about 3.3 mm each, allowing for gatefolds; about 90 packed tight. Two drawers: about 170.
  • Singles compartment: about 17 cm wide behind a 12 mm divider: about 60 singles spine-up at the back, inner sleeves at the front.
  • Runners: 400 mm full-extension ball-bearing runners rated 68–70 kg per pair (45 kg is the minimum), screwed straight into the carcass's 18 mm ply sides. The whole box clears the unit, so the back records are as easy to reach as the front.
  • Weight and safety: about 30 kg per full drawer. The carcass must be fixed to the wall with anti-tip brackets before the drawers are loaded, and only one drawer opened at a time.

How to build them

  1. Build the carcass: glue and screw the top and bottom between the sides, square, with the two back rails. Have the merchant cut the ply to the list; it is the easiest part of the wall to get wrong by hand.
  2. Set it in place beside bay 2 on a flat floor, shim it level with the frames' tops at 73, bolt it to bay 2's frame, and fix it to the wall with anti-tip brackets.
  3. Fit the runners straight onto the ply sides, level, every screw hole used: the lower pair so the box underside sits at 2.3 cm, the upper pair at 37.0 cm. Make the box width exactly what the runner instructions specify (normally the opening less 25 mm).
  4. Build the boxes: sides, back and low front panel glued and screwed onto the 18 mm base. Fix the divider 33 cm in from the left inside face, the raked rest at the back of the trough, and glue the felt down.
  5. Hang the boxes and check they run true empty.
  6. Fit the oak lips from inside the box, with a routed finger pull at the top centre, 2–3 mm clear of the face frame.
  7. Load gradually, one drawer at a time, and check both still run smoothly full.
