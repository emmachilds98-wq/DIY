Build guide: the whole wall

Everything needed to build the unit, shelves, drawers, PC shelf and the slat wall. Sizes are finished sizes in centimetres. Measure your own alcove first: walls are rarely square, so take its width at the floor, at 76 cm, at 192 cm and at the ceiling, and cut the worktop and shelves to the smallest.

Tools

  • Drill-driver and an SDS or hammer drill for the masonry, with masonry bits and plugs.
  • Spirit level (a cross-line laser makes the shelf heights quick), tape, combination square, stud and cable detector.
  • Track or circular saw for the worktop and ply (or have the timber merchant cut the ply and oak to the cut lists), jigsaw for notches, mitre saw for the slats.
  • Router with a cove bit for the finger pulls (or buy lips with the pull routed), clamps, sander, wood glue.
  • Dust mask, eye and ear protection.

Order of work

  1. Check and measure: ceiling height, alcove width at four heights, the walls for plumb, the floor for level across the alcove, and your records and games.
  2. Electrician: Cat6 runs, the double socket in the alcove, sockets by the sofa and under the PC shelf, and the fused spur for future LEDs. Mark every cable route on the wall before painting.
  3. Paint the room, alcove included, and let it cure for a few days before fixing anything to it.
  4. Mark heights on the walls with a level or laser: 73 (worktop battens), 37 (kit shelf battens), 109 (turntable shelf underside), 152 (left shelf underside), 188 (high shelf underside).
  5. Fix the cleats and battens for the three oak shelves and the sub bay, into the masonry, level.
  6. Build the plinth and set it against the spacer blocks, shimmed dead level: it decides whether the worktop sits flat.
  7. Assemble the frames with their back panels off, stand them on the plinth, join them with BESTÅ connectors and screw them down to it.
  8. Build the bay 3 carcass, set it beside bay 2, shim it so its top is level with the frames at 73, bolt it to bay 2, and fit the anti-tip brackets on all three.
  9. Fit the worktop: scribe the ends to the walls if needed, seal every cut edge, and screw up through the frame and carcass tops (short screws that can't come through). Felt pads on the sub-bay battens.
  10. Fit the trunking in the channel behind the unit, mains on one side and data on the other, and run the cables in.
  11. Fit the internal shelves in bays 1 and 2 at the fit-check heights.
  12. Build and fit the drawers: both record drawers in bay 3, then the game drawer in bay 1 (steps in their sections).
  13. Fit the kit shelf and the hidden drawer in the sub bay, and put the sub on its riser.
  14. Hang the oak shelves on their cleats: high shelf, turntable shelf, left shelf. Screw up through the cleats into each shelf so it can't lift.
  15. Build the PC shelf box, fit the keyboard tray runners, hang it on its cleat and brackets, fit the screen and the trunking down to the skirting.
  16. Make the LP browser, and finish all the oak with hardwax oil.
  17. Place and connect everything, label each plug and HDMI input, and test.

The slat wall can go up before step 14 if you'd rather have the shelves fitted against finished slats; either order works, as the slats are cut to run between the shelves.

Then: slat wall, face frame and lighting

  1. Battens: fix 25 × 50 mm battens horizontally across the back wall at about 79, 115, 155 and 205 cm, plus short ones above the turntable and left shelves. Leave 5 cm gaps where cables run up the right side and behind the TV.
  2. Wiring: run the LED cables and Deco lead in the gaps, to drivers on the fused spur in the sub bay.
  3. Slats: the wall needs 225 cm of width, three full panels and one cut to 45 cm, from the worktop (75.8) to the high shelf's underside (188) and from its top (192) to the ceiling: about 140 cm from each 240 cm panel, cut around the turntable and left shelves. The offcuts make the PC screen. Screw through the felt between the slats into the battens.
  4. Face frame: cut the MDF stiles and rails, glue and pin them to the frame and carcass fronts, scribe the end stiles to the walls, fill, prime and paint in the soft green eggshell. Openings start at 11.6 cm in bays 1–2 (the bottom rail hides the plinth) and at 2.6 cm in bay 3 and the sub bay; the top rail runs from 71 to 73. Check all three drawer lips clear it by 2–3 mm.
  5. LEDs: aluminium channels under the front edges of the three oak shelves and under the worktop in each bay; bias light on the back of the TV, powered from its USB port.
