If the collection grows

  • Overflow cube unit: a 1 × 4 or 2 × 4 cube unit such as IKEA KALLAX (cubes about 33 cm inside, 39 cm deep) holds about 60–70 LPs per cube. On its side behind the sofa or under a window, a 2 × 4 holds about 500 LPs and gives you a second display top.
  • Tighter packing: each tray takes about 95 LPs packed tight, at the cost of an easy flip.
  • LP browser depth: keep it face-out at 24 cm deep. A deeper rack under the turntable would weigh about 35 kg over the sub bay, more than the worktop should carry there.

Paint: Little Greene Sage Green

The room is colour-drenched in Little Greene Sage Green No. 80, your pick from the last round: walls, ceiling, alcove, skirting, door and frame, radiator and the PC trunking, all one colour. It's a muted, desaturated green with a calm, classic feel, warmer and a step deeper than the grey-greens, and it sets the oak off well. The oak, the records and the warm light do the contrast work.

  • Finishes: Absolute Matt or Intelligent Matt emulsion on walls, ceiling and alcove; Intelligent Eggshell, in the same colour, on skirting, door, frame and trunking. It wipes clean and gives the woodwork a slight sheen against the walls.
  • Ceiling: the same colour keeps the drench seamless. If it ever feels low, Little Greene's pale neutrals such as Portland Stone Pale are suggested for ceilings alongside Sage Green.
  • For the OLED: Sage Green is a mid-tone, a little lighter than the deep green but still matt and muted. Dim the ceiling light while watching and keep the bias light behind the TV.
  • Choosing: paint an A4 board and look at it by day and with the lamps and LED strip on, since warm LEDs push it towards olive. If budget matters, a trade counter can usually colour-match it into a cheaper range.
  • Order: paint before building anything, alcove included, so nothing needs cutting in around the uprights and shelves.
  • How much: measure walls and ceiling. A mid green over pale walls usually covers in two coats after a mist coat.

Skirting: paint it Sage Green too

  • Recommended: Sage Green No. 80 in Intelligent Eggshell. The same colour as the walls with a soft sheen. It completes the drench, so the room's edges disappear and the oak base and records are what you notice. It's also the most forgiving with scuffs from feet and the hoover.
  • Darker option: Little Greene Obsidian Green. Suggested as the deep partner for Sage Green: a near-black green that grounds the room and hides scuffs. It works if you want a defined edge, but it draws the eye down.
  • Keeping a light trim: swap the bright white for a warm off-white such as Portland Stone Pale, also suggested with Sage Green for trims. Brilliant white next to a muted green looks cold and stark.
  • Painting over white gloss or satin: wash, key with a fine sanding pad, prime with a primer made for woodwork, then two coats of eggshell. Do the door, frame and window board at the same time.
  • Behind the unit: where the base meets the left wall inside the alcove, either cut the skirting back so the oak end stile sits flush to the wall, or scribe the stile over it.

Lighting

Three layers, all warm: the LED strip you already have round the ceiling, light built into the oak, and one ceiling light in the middle of the room.

Now: the LED strip round the ceiling

  • What you have: a strip stuck to the ceiling round the whole edge of the room, facing down. In the renders it runs along the left wall, into the alcove, across its back wall and reveal, and across the chimney breast, washing the top of every wall.
  • Where it meets the oak: the uprights run up to the ceiling against the back wall, so the strip would stop at each one. Cut a small notch, about 2 × 1.5 cm, in the back top corner of each upright so the strip runs straight through.
  • Keep it warm: 2700K, on a dimmer, so it matches the rest.

The ceiling light: a paper lantern

Hung in line with the alcove's right-hand edge, out in the middle of the room. A flattened oval washi-paper lantern suits the room best: handmade paper on a bamboo frame sits with the hand-cut oak joinery and the cane PC screen, and it gives soft, even light with no hard reflection in the OLED.
