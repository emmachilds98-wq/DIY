Views

Speed

  • A picture while it loadsShow the whole-room still straight away and swap in the live room when it's ready, so the tab never opens on a blank box.
    Done in v22
  • Fewer things to drawJoin the pieces that never move (the media wall's insides, the record sleeves, the plants' pots) into one shape per material, as the walls already are. v22: 433 draws a frame down to about 196 (your plant stand alone went from 152 pieces to 7); a frame takes about a third less time.
    Done in v22
  • A light budgetEach of the 23 lights is worked out for every surface. v22: in Fast each spot bar lights with two of its four heads at twice the strength, and the area lights stay off; HD keeps every light for pictures.
    Done in v22
  • Share materialsMany parts make their own copy of the same oak or paint, which gives the graphics card 66 recipes to prepare at the start. Sharing them shortens the wait before the room appears.
    Later
  • Softer shadows without the costBake the soft shadows under fixed furniture into the floor once, and keep live shadows for the lights you're adjusting.
    Later

The page

  • Fold the 3D controls into five drawersOver 110 buttons sit in one panel nine phone-screens long. Group them as Layout (table, chairs, plant stand), Lights, Look (paint, rug, curtains, cushions), Extras (ideas and suggestions) and Views. Only one drawer is open at a time and the 3D picture stays in sight while you choose.
    Done in v22
  • Build steps one at a time67 phone screens is too long to use on site. Show each step folded to its title and a tick, open one at a time, with “next step” at the bottom and your progress at the top. The ticks already save.
    Done in v22
  • A contents list in the long tabsThe Media wall, Checks, Dining and Network tabs get a short list of their sections at the top that jumps to each one.
    Next
  • “Your picks”One list of the ideas, suggestions and decor you've switched on, with their rough cost added up, and a button to send them as a note for the next round, so a favourite can move into the plan.
    Done in v22
  • A start-here path on the overviewFour steps with where you are on each: see it in 3D, answer what's still open (three questions now), buy, build. Each comes from what's already saved: your answers, the buy-list ticks and the build ticks.
    Later
  • A lighter shared plan pageIts pictures are built into the page, making it 10.8 MB. Moving them into the page's own picture store would bring it to about 2 MB, so it opens much faster on a phone. Nothing you see changes.
    Done in v22
  • A shorter PDFThe plan PDF is 79 pages, a good part of it the history of earlier rounds. Keep the history on the web page only and end the PDF at the sources.
    Later

Furniture and decor

  • Six suggestions, each on or offA tall floor plant and a round oak side table at the sofa's open end, a knitted pouffe on the rug, a large print over the sofa's return, a clock over the door, and a tray with books and a candle on the coffee table. All are off to start, with All on and All off buttons. The floor pieces move in Edit room, and anything you remove there comes back when you switch it on. About £190–445 for all six, and none of it is in the plan or the costs.
    Done in v21
  • More to add in Edit roomA rattan armchair, an oak blanket ladder, an oak bench and an arc floor lamp join the Add list (it already had an armchair, side table, floor plant, bookcase, beanbag, footstool, floor cushion, record crate, floor vase, pouffe, basket, mirror and lantern).
    Done in v21
  • Choices for each suggestionTwo or three versions of each: a fig, a monstera or an olive tree; an oak or a black side table; three prints. Like the rug and curtain choices.

Behind the scenes

  • Only re-render the stills that changedEvery round renders all 27 HD stills of the 3D room, about 20 minutes. Keeping a fingerprint of each still's settings would skip the ones that haven't changed.
    Next
  • Fewer backup filesThe repository holds about 130 backup copies of the scripts from earlier rounds. Git already keeps every version, so new backups could stay out of the repository, and the old ones could move into one folder.
